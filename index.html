Our mission

To give every driver the confidence to understand, maintain and protect their vehicle through intelligent AI guidance.

Our vision is to become the world's most trusted AI companion for vehicle ownership — the first thing you turn to for any car problem, and the quiet nudge that keeps the routine stuff (MOT, servicing, insurance) from ever catching you out.

What we stand for

Trust, clarity, honesty, confidence, safety, transparency and empowerment. No jargon, no panic, no confusion — just clear answers, explained the way a trusted friend would explain them.

About the founder

Why SafVia exists

SafVia was founded by Siama Qadar after a frightening experience with her own car. It was only three and a half years old, and it kept stopping working without warning. She jump-started it and it ran again — until a few days later, the radio began showing strange warning symptoms, and then the car died a second time.

Not knowing what was wrong, whether it was safe to drive, or who to trust for an honest answer was the hardest part — more stressful than the fault itself. She wanted a way to understand what was happening to her own car, get pointed towards the right help, and walk into a garage or call for assistance feeling informed rather than anxious.

SafVia is built to be that first, calm, honest voice — so no driver has to feel as lost as she did that day.

Privacy policy

Draft — please have this reviewed by a solicitor/data protection professional before relying on it, particularly around UK GDPR compliance.

What we collect

Name and email address (when you sign up), and any car symptom descriptions or garage quotes you choose to submit to our AI tools. Vehicle details and your diary history are stored only in your own browser, not on SafVia's servers.

Why we collect it

To operate the free plan, send you reminders about your car (MOT, servicing), and to generate AI guidance in response to what you type.

Who we share it with

Submissions to the symptom checker and garage translator are sent to Anthropic (our AI provider) to generate a response. Name/email sign-ups are processed via Netlify Forms. Our site is hosted by Netlify. We do not sell your data to advertisers.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by contacting us (see Contact). You can also clear your vehicle and diary data yourself at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies

SafVia does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to remember your vehicle details and whether you've signed up.
